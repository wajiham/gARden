# 🌿 AR Garden — Social Augmented Reality Experience

**Design. Grow. Connect. Compete.**

AR Garden is a university group project exploring how **augmented reality, virtual gardening, and social interaction** can come together in an Android app. The goal is to let users place 3D plants in their surroundings, care for a virtual garden, connect with friends, and compare their gardens.

> **Project status:** 🚧 In development. The features below describe the planned application and may not yet be implemented.

## ✨ Planned Features

### 🌱 AR Garden
- Place 3D plants on detected real-world surfaces.
- Move, rotate, resize, and remove plants.
- Save and revisit a virtual garden.

### 💧 Plant Care
- Track plant health and species-specific watering schedules.
- Water individual plants using a limited water supply and cooldown system.
- Earn garden points by keeping plants healthy.

### 👥 Social Features
- Register, sign in, and manage a user profile.
- Send, accept, and reject friend requests.
- View friends' saved gardens.

### ⌚ Wear OS Integration
- Receive watering reminders on a smartwatch.
- Check plants that need attention and water selected plants from the watch.

### 🏆 Possible Future Enhancements
- Garden competitions, leaderboards, and voting.
- Additional garden interactions and seasonal effects.

## 🏗️ Project Modules

The three-person team is organizing development around:

1. **Users & Social** — Authentication, profiles, friendships, and friend-garden navigation.
2. **AR Garden** — Plant placement, editing, and saved layouts.
3. **Plant Care & Watch** — Watering, health, reminders, and smartwatch interactions.

Shared data models and repositories will allow the modules to communicate.

## 🚀 Getting Started

Setup and installation instructions will be added once the Android project structure and dependencies are finalized.

To run the application when available, you will need Android Studio and a compatible Android device. AR functionality requires a device supported by ARCore; smartwatch features require a compatible Wear OS environment.
